# Ghost Hour

A haunted-manor platformer for the Commodore 64. Written in 6502 assembly (ACME), with graphics,
level data and music generated by small Python tools. Written for a stock PAL C64 and tested in the
VICE emulator.

[Watch the demo video](demo/ghost_hour_demo.mp4): from the title screen to the rescue in the Bedroom.

The great clock stopped one minute before midnight, so the hour of the ghosts never ended. You are
a telegram runner in a yellow slicker, caught in the storm. Find the four lost clock parts and bring
them to the clock in the Great Hall.

## Playing

    ./run.sh               # builds if needed, opens VICE

| Action                        | Joystick (port 2) | Keyboard |
|-------------------------------|-------------------|----------|
| Walk                          | left / right      | A / D    |
| Jump, climb up                | up                | W        |
| Climb down                    | down              | S        |
| Raise the candle (burns wax)  | fire              | SPACE    |

- **Your candle is your light.** Indoors you only see what it lights, and it dims as the wax burns.
  Wax does not burn outdoors. Lightning briefly shows the whole room.
- **In the dark you only see eyes.** Raising the candle drives off ghosts, bats and wolves nearby,
  but burns wax much faster.
- **Ghosts** drift through walls towards you and cost 1 nerve. **Bats** steal wax instead.
  **Werewolves** patrol the floor and cost 2 nerve. At zero nerve you faint.
- **Candles** refill your wax. The one in the Great Hall comes back each time you re-enter.
- In the dark, a white twinkle marks a clock part and a yellow one a candle. The message row tells you
  when a clock part is in the room. Walk into a part to take it.
- **Brooke** is trapped on the top plank in the Bedroom, with the clock's winding key. Stand beside her
  and hold fire or SPACE for about two seconds. Afterwards she waits by the clock in the Great Hall.

## Building

Tested on Ubuntu 24.04. Nothing is installed system-wide and sudo is not needed: the setup script puts
ACME, VICE, the C64 ROMs that VICE needs and a Python venv in `.tools/`. It needs `curl`, `apt-get`,
`dpkg` and `python3` with `venv`. That folder is not part of this repository.

    ./setup.sh      # once: fetches the toolchain into .tools/
    make            # build/ghosthour.prg and build/ghosthour.d64
    make debug      # build/ghosthour_debug.prg, with test hooks
    make clean

`build/ghosthour.d64` also works, but loading through the emulated 1541 takes about two minutes
unless you turn on warp. `./run.sh` injects the PRG directly and starts at once.

## How it is put together

    src/main.asm          the game: raster splits, lighting, physics, enemies, HUD, SID player
    tools/rooms.py        the 13 rooms, as ASCII maps, plus their items and enemies   <- edit levels here
    tools/tiles.py        tile art and flags
    tools/sprites.py      sprite art
    tools/music.py        the two tunes
    tools/title.py        draws the title picture and fits it to the bitmap colour limits
    tools/font.py         HUD font and icons
    tools/check_map.py    checks that every room and item can be reached, and that you can get back
    tools/build_assets.py turns all of the above into build/*.bin and build/gen.asm
    tools/preview.py      renders rooms and sprites to PNG, no emulator needed
    tools/shot.py         drives a debug build in VICE with scripted input and takes screenshots
    tools/record_demo.py  a bot plays from the title to Brooke's rescue and records demo/ghost_hour_demo.mp4

How the lighting works: every character has a lit version (code N) and a dark version (code N+128),
where the wall colour drops to black and detail becomes one dim colour. Each frame, only the cells
whose state changed are redrawn, one row span at a time, so a step costs very little raster time.
Lightning is a single write to the background colour. Sprites use two layers, a hi-res ink outline
over a multicolour fill, to get the look of inked comic art.
